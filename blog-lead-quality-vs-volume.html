Lead Quality vs Lead Volume:
Which Should You Optimise For?

More leads isn't automatically better

A funnel that doubles lead volume while halving conversion rate hasn't actually improved anything — it's just shifted work downstream to your sales team, who now sort through more unqualified contacts.

Define "qualified" before you optimise

Lead quality is often treated as vague, but it should be a specific, measurable definition — budget range, timeline, or a particular behaviour on your site — before any campaign optimisation begins.

Volume still matters at certain stages

Early-stage brands with limited data sometimes need volume first, simply to have enough leads to identify what "qualified" actually looks like for their specific offer.

Where the two goals conflict

Broader targeting and lower-friction forms tend to increase volume but dilute quality; narrower targeting and more qualifying questions do the reverse. The right balance depends on your sales team's capacity to follow up.

Tracking the right metric

Cost per qualified lead, not cost per lead, is usually the number worth watching — it accounts for both volume and quality in a single figure your team can act on.
