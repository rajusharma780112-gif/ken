Avoiding Ad Account Bans
in Trading & iGaming.

Why these accounts get flagged more often

Major ad platforms apply stricter, often opaque, review processes to trading and gambling-adjacent brands. A single policy violation can result in an account suspension that takes weeks to resolve, if it's resolved at all.

Read the actual policy, not the summary

Platform advertising policies for financial products and gambling are detailed and change periodically. Relying on a general understanding rather than the current policy text is one of the most common causes of avoidable suspensions.

Landing page consistency matters

Ad platforms review not just the ad creative but the landing page it points to. A mismatch between what the ad promises and what the landing page delivers is a common trigger for review flags.

Build account structure with resilience in mind

Spreading campaigns across properly structured accounts, rather than relying on a single account for all activity, reduces the impact if one does get flagged for review.

Have a response plan ready

When an account is flagged, a fast, accurate appeal — referencing the specific policy and how your campaign complies — resolves issues faster than a generic response.
