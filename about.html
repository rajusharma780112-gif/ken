ABOUT US

Marketing built for
real, measurable growth.

Kentek Media is a performance marketing agency built around one idea — every campaign should be judged on results you can actually measure, not vanity metrics.

Who we work with

We work across Trading, FMCG, Fintech, Fashion, Finance, Events and Wellness brands. Every industry has its own audience behaviour and channel mix — we don't run the same playbook for a trading platform as we do for a wellness product, and our strategy reflects that from day one.

How we work

We don't believe in one-size-fits-all packages. Every engagement starts with an audit of where you actually stand — your current SEO position, ad account history, and lead flow — before we recommend anything. That audit shapes the strategy, not a template.

What makes our approach different

Three things guide every campaign we run:

  • Strategy before spend. We map out the approach and expected outcomes before a single ad goes live, not after.
  • Transparent reporting. You see the same numbers we're looking at internally — no dashboards designed to look busy instead of clear.
  • Results over vanity metrics. Traffic and impressions matter less to us than qualified leads and actual return on ad spend.

Who we work best with

We're the right fit for brands that want a genuine growth partner, not just a vendor executing a fixed list of deliverables — across food and beverage, retail, tech, fashion, finance, events and wellness.
